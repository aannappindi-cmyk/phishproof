Phishing awareness for student organizations

Spot the scam before it spots you.

PhishProof is a phishing awareness platform for student organizations. Members practice spotting realistic fake emails and texts aimed at college students, click the red flags for instant feedback, and club officers track which scams trip people up most. The goal is to protect students — who are frequent scam targets — with just a few minutes of hands-on practice.

How it works

Short, realistic drills instead of long, boring trainings.

📩

Realistic scenarios

Fake financial aid notices, job offers, textbook deals, and "your account is locked" texts modeled on scams students actually get.

🚩

Click the red flags

Tap suspicious senders, links, and urgent language to get instant feedback explaining why each one is a warning sign.

📊

Officer dashboard

Club officers see which scams trip members up most, so they know what to cover at the next meeting.
